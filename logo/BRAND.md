# Teal brand mark: the tern

The mark is a tern in flight, one solid shape, no outline, no gradient inside the bird. It flies to the right.

Files
- tern-ink.svg / tern-mint.svg / tern-white.svg: the bird alone, transparent background. Use ink (#0B1218) on light,
  mint (#33DDC4) or white on dark.
- app-icon-mint.svg: the app icon as shipped (mint square, radius 23%, ink tern). app-icon-dark.svg: the dark variant.
- app-icon-128.png: raster of the app icon.

Colours
- Mint #33DDC4 (accent; app icon background, gradient to #16B49E allowed on the icon square only)
- Ink #0B1218 (text on light, the bird on the icon)
- Navy #243240 → #0B1218 (dark surfaces)
- Paper #ECF0EE (light surfaces)
- Amber #F59E0B (warnings), Rose #F43F5E (errors)

Type
- Headings: Bricolage Grotesque (semibold). Body and UI: Instrument Sans.

Rules
- Keep clear space around the bird equal to its wing height. Minimum size 16 px: at that size use the icon square, not the bare bird.
- Never add a hand, a shield, a lock or a globe to the bird. Never stretch, outline, rotate or mirror it.
- A sister product mark (Tealhand) may use the same tern, the same mint/ink/paper, and its own simple second shape next to
  the bird or in place of the square, so the family reads as one.
