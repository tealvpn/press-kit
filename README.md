# Teal VPN press kit

Everything a creator or journalist needs to talk about Teal VPN. Free to use as is; please do not alter the icon.
Contact: Sameer Chokly, Fxolio LLC — sameer@fxolio.com — https://tealvpn.com

## In one sentence
Teal VPN is a private VPN with a free plan (500 MB a day, no card, no ads), built for hotel, airport, campus and office
Wi-Fi that filters or blocks VPN traffic.

## Facts (October 2026)
- Made by Fxolio LLC, a software company registered in Wyoming, USA. Launched 2026.
- Apps: Android (Google Play and direct APK), Windows 10/11 (Intel, AMD, ARM), Mac (macOS 13+, Apple silicon and Intel),
  Linux (Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Intel/AMD/ARM), Android TV / Google TV, Chromebook. iPhone/iPad in testing.
- Installs: Google Play · tealvpn.com downloads · `winget install Fxolio.TealVPN` · `choco install teal-vpn` · Scoop bucket ·
  `brew install --cask tealvpn/tap/teal-vpn` · `sudo apt install teal-vpn` (signed repository).
- Teal Free: 500 MB of data a day, one location (Lithuania), up to 2 devices, no payment details, no ads.
- Teal Pro: unlimited data, every location (Europe, North America, Asia, Australia; 14 countries incl. a Delhi location),
  up to 3 devices. $7.99 a month, $24 for 6 months, $36 a year ($3 a month). 7-day trial on monthly and yearly. US prices.
- Connection: "Teal Secure", an obfuscated protocol that is harder to identify on filtering networks; the app switches to
  another route by itself when one stalls; kill switch; split tunnelling on Windows.
- Privacy: no logs of websites visited, destinations or traffic contents; connection IP addresses are not stored; the
  private key is created on the device and never sent to Teal. Policies: https://tealvpn.com/privacy ·
  https://tealvpn.com/transparency
- Sign-in: Google, Apple or email. One account on all devices.

blocking.
